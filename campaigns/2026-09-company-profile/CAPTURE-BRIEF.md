## 0. The one photographic rule that needs stating up front

`CLAUDE.md` says **hard light, stark contrast, backdrops must be brand colours, no neutral grey.** That rule was written for *set-built* imagery — studio product, character composites, poster art — and it stays binding there.

**It cannot bind documentary photography, and it should not.** A real Banani shopfront at dusk does not have a cyan backdrop, and putting one behind it would make the estate look like a render. The credibility of this document comes from the pictures being obviously real.

So the profile runs **two visual layers**, and they must not be blended:

| Layer | Where it applies | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Set-built** | Product, packaging, Surprise Card, character composites, portraits against seamless | Full brand rules. Hard directional light, crisp shadow, brand-colour backdrop, palette-exact |
| **Documentary** | Outlets, cart, kitchen, team at work, customers, events, Singapore | Shoot the truth. Favour hard, directional daylight and real signage light. Graded *toward* the palette in post — cyan and pink lifted, cocoa in the shadows instead of black. Never staged into a brand backdrop |

**Grade note for the retoucher:** documentary frames are graded to sit next to the palette, not to match it. Shadows land on Deep Cocoa `#450001`, never pure black. Do not push saturation until the food goes false — a waffle that looks like a render defeats the purpose of shooting it.

**Never colour-match to the packaging.** `assets/marketing/packaging/BD BOX A.jpg` prints in a hotter magenta/mint that is off-palette. The box appears in frame; it does not set the grade.

## 1. Technical specs

**Stills**
- RAW + full-size JPEG, both retained. Minimum 24 MP.
- Every hero subject shot **both landscape and portrait**. The profile is 16:9 landscape, but the same frames will be re-cropped for social, decks and print for years.
- Deliberate negative space on at least one frame per subject — pages need somewhere for the headline to sit.
- Colour target card in the first frame of every lighting setup. Non-negotiable for cross-location consistency.
- Locked-off tripod for all exteriors and interiors; handheld only for candid and craft work.

**Motion** *(capture while on site — the marginal cost is near zero and it feeds a 60–90s profile sizzle plus a year of social)*
- 4K, **30 fps** (brand standard), 10-bit if the body supports it.
- 6–10 second takes minimum, locked-off and slow-move versions of every hero setup.
- Shoot 9:16 safe: keep the subject inside a centre vertical crop.
- Room tone and real kitchen sound at every location. The crunch is a brand asset — record it clean.

**Naming**
`WUP26-<SHOT-ID>-<location-slug>-<nnn>.<ext>` — e.g. `WUP26-EST-01-banani-014.CR3`

**Delivery**
Selects graded to the palette; full RAW archive retained. Lands in `assets/photography/` under the same section folders as the shot IDs below. This folder does not exist yet — it will be the first new top-level addition to the asset library.

## 11. Permissions, releases and access

Sort these **before** the crew moves, not on the day.

- **Model releases** — every identifiable staff member and every customer. Bilingual form, signed on the day. Anyone under 18 needs a guardian signature.
- **Mall and landlord permission** — several sites sit inside malls and complexes that prohibit tripods and commercial shooting without written clearance. This is the most common cause of a lost shoot day.
- **Delivery-partner marks** — do not shoot a Foodpanda- or Pathao-branded bag, rider or storefront decal as a hero unless the partner has cleared it. Own-delivery kit is unrestricted.
- **Kitchen access** — food-safety sign-off, and shoot outside peak service. A kitchen crew being filmed during the dinner rush produces bad pictures and worse service.
- **Music and audio** — nothing in `assets/marketing/audio/` has a verified commercial-rights record. It cannot go into any public cut of a profile video without human sign-off.
- **Event venue consent** — separate from mall consent, and usually the organiser's to give.

## 12. Never in frame

- **Off-palette or damaged signage** — photograph it as a repair job, not as brand imagery.
- **Any delivery mark at a Chef's Table site.** Chef's Table has no delivery. A Foodpanda decal in a CT frame is a factual error printed in a company profile.
- **A stale menu board.** Verify against the current price book before the shutter fires.
- A visible price anywhere, unless it has been confirmed against the right price book for that channel.
- Competitor branding, or a rival product in the background.
- **Anything AI-generated.** No AI logo, no AI character, no AI product in a hero or claim position, no AI packaging. This document's entire job is to be verifiable. AI belongs in mood boards for this project and nowhere else.
- Alcohol adjacency, or any framing that reads as suggestive. Primary audience starts at 10.
