# One More Block — 20s reel

**Status:** voice test. Written 31 Aug 2026 at Jawat's request, for ElevenLabs experimentation.
**Cast:** Bhoppu · Air Maxi · Mr Waffle
**Territory:** *No occasion required* (late night) + *the handle*
**Promise it serves:** joy that travels

> Two standing rules this deliberately tests. `CLAUDE.md` §5 says **one character per piece**, and
> character dialogue is **DRAFT until signed off**. This script is three-handed and fully spoken —
> which is exactly the material that needs Jawat's sign-off. Treat the recording as the audition.

---

## 1. The idea

Air Maxi is walking everyone somewhere. Bhoppu is complaining, but still walking. Mr Waffle
closes it in five words. Nobody sells anything; the joke is that they both showed up for
different reasons and it worked anyway.

The three voices are the three movement languages — **motion, mass, stillness** — which is why
this is a good first voice test: if the three reads don't sound obviously different, the casting
is wrong.

---

## 2. Script — 20 seconds

| # | Time | Who | Line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0 – 3.2 | **Air Maxi** | "One more block. Come on — it's still open." |
| 2 | 3.2 – 7.3 | **Bhoppu** | "One more block? *(puff)* You said that four blocks ago." |
| 3 | 7.3 – 9.8 | **Air Maxi** | "And you're still going. That's the whole point." |
| 4 | 9.8 – 14.4 | **Bhoppu** | "I'm going because there's a waffle at the end. Different reason. Same direction." |
| 5 | 15.0 – 17.4 | **Mr Waffle** | *(beat)* "…You're both here. It worked." |
| 6 | 18.2 – 19.6 | **Mr Waffle** | "Stay crispy." |

~17s of speech, ~3s of air. The gaps are load-bearing — Mr Waffle's beat before line 5 is the
joke.

**Bangla alternate for line 4** (Bhoppu, if you want the code-switch on the test):
> "Ami jacchi karon shesh e waffle ache. Different reason. Same direction."

## 3. Voice direction for ElevenLabs

Start from Voice Design (text-to-voice) with these prompts, then tune. Slider values are
starting points, not settings — expect to move them.

### Bhoppu — the mass
> **Voice Design prompt:** "A big, warm, friendly young man in his twenties. Mid-low chest voice,
> rounded and slightly husky, a little out of breath. Cheerful, unbothered, completely without
> edge. Conversational, never performed."

- **Stability** ~0.35 (low — the breathiness needs to wander) · **Similarity** ~0.75 ·
  **Style** ~0.45 · **Speed** slightly under 1.0
- Delivery: he is *genuinely* walking. Breath before "You said that", and a small laugh under
  "Different reason."
- v3 audio tags to try: `[out of breath]` before line 2, `[laughs softly]` before "Different reason."
- **Never** let him sound pathetic, self-pitying or wheezy-comic. He is the most likeable one
  in the cast and he knows it.

### Air Maxi — the motion
> **Voice Design prompt:** "A confident, quick, upbeat young man, late teens to early twenties.
> Bright mid-to-high register with a light rasp. Forward-leaning, encouraging, urban. Speaks in
> short bursts with rising energy. Not shouting."

- **Stability** ~0.45 · **Similarity** ~0.75 · **Style** ~0.55 · **Speed** ~1.1
- Delivery: line 1 is an invitation, not an order — the "come on" is warm. Line 3 lands on
  **"you're"**, not on "I". He is pointing at the standard, never at Bhoppu.
- v3 tag to try: `[excited]` on line 1.
- **Never** smug, never possessive, never a superlative claim about the brand.

### Mr Waffle — the stillness
> **Voice Design prompt:** "A calm, dry, unhurried man in his thirties. Low, even, minimal
> inflection, faintly amused. Speaks slowly and stops early. Deadpan, warm underneath."

- **Stability** ~0.75 (high — flatness *is* the character) · **Similarity** ~0.8 ·
  **Style** ~0.15 · **Speed** ~0.85
- Delivery: the ellipsis before "You're both here" is a real pause — hold it. "It worked" is
  a statement of fact, not a punchline. "Stay crispy" is thrown away, not announced.
- v3 tag to try: `[sighs]` before line 5 — test it, it may be too much.
- **Never** an intensifier, never an exclamation, never explains the joke.

**Mixing note:** cut all three to roughly equal loudness, then pull Mr Waffle down ~1.5 dB.
He should feel like the quietest thing on the track and still be the one you hear.

## 4. Sound

- **Street, late.** Thin traffic, a distant horn, footsteps under lines 1–4. Dhaka at 1am, not
  a busy road.
- **The crunch is the hero sound.** One clean bite, dry and loud, in the gap at 17.4–18.2 —
  directly before "Stay crispy."
- Footsteps stop on Mr Waffle's beat. Silence is what makes his line land.
- House track *Sweet Night Bites* can sit underneath at low level — **rights are unverified in
  this kit, so not for publication without approval.**
