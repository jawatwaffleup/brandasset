# Brand Asset Catalog

This kit is a curated, portable working library. It combines the original brand kit with selected production-ready material from the adjacent WaffleUp source folders.

## Character source of truth

| Market | Roster | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Bangladesh | Mr Waffle, Air Maxi, Bhoppu, Picchi, Stovy, Swirly, Tvy, Spacy, Icy | `assets/characters/bangladesh/` |
| Singapore | Bangladesh nine plus Merlulu | `assets/characters/singapore/merlulu/` |

There are **four** supplied printable 3D character models: Mr Waffle, Air Maxi, Bhoppu and Picchi. Each is stored under its character folder as `3d/model.stl`.

## Added material

| Local location | Contents |
|---|---|
| `assets/characters/bangladesh/` | Organised canonical 2D art, the nine-character lineup and the four STL models |
| `assets/characters/singapore/merlulu/` | Merlulu Surprise Card EPS, SG sticker-sheet AI and JPG reference art |
| `assets/marketing/` | Curated event, packaging, product, Surprise Card, social-source and included audio assets |
| `docs/brand-guides/` | Original Brand Book V.1, Brand Guideline 1 and 2024 Company Profile PDFs |

## Intentional boundary

The original Google Takeout remains the archive for large raw animation projects, production video masters, full photography libraries, campaign working files, print-production files and non-canonical character art. The four requested printable 3D models and the Merlulu source artwork are exceptions and are included here.

The included house-track audio has no verified commercial-rights record. Obtain human approval before publishing it.
