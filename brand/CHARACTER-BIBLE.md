## 0. What this file is, and what it replaces

This is the **single source of truth for who these characters are** — archetype, motivation, movement, silhouette, relationships, powers and visual rules.

| File | Holds | Precedence |
|---|---|---|
| **`CHARACTER-BIBLE.md`** (this) | Who they are and how they move | **Highest.** Everything else defers. |
| `character-voices/*.md` | How they *speak* only | Defers to this file |
| `character-voices/CAST-MAP.md` | Keeping voices distinct | Defers to this file |
| `CHARACTERS.md` | Printed card bios, asset inventory | Historical record of what's printed |
| `data/characters.json` | Machine-readable card bios | Historical record of what's printed |

**The card bios are marketing copy on a physical object, not a character spec.** Several of them were over-read when the voice files were first written — most damagingly Bhoppu's *"elevated spots"*, which produced an entire personality about climbing that no one intended. Where a card bio and this file disagree, **this file wins and the card bio is treated as a quote of a printed artefact.**

## 2. The cast

### 1 · Bhoppu — the lovable food obsessive
> *Loves food more than anything. Goes to the gym anyway. Loses, every time, happily.*

**Reference energy:** Kung Fu Panda.
**Motivation:** Food. Not health, not strength — food. He will do a workout if a waffle is the reward, and then eat the reward mid-workout.
**Energy 5/5 · Warmth 5/5**

**Movement & silhouette.** Enormous top-heavy cyan mass on small feet. Always leaning back to counterweight the belly. Comedic walk — heavy, rolling, slightly out of breath. He is the **mass** of the group: the physical anchor other characters stack on, lean against or bounce off.

**The running gag, drawn.** In every pose the dumbbell and the food are in **opposite hands**. Jogging with a measuring tape round the middle, waffle in hand · flexing a bicep with a waffle in the raised fist · exercise bike with a shake · face-down mid-push-up with a waffle in the thought bubble · collapsed in a heap with an ice cream. That structure — effort and appetite in the same frame, never resolving — **is** the character.

**Signatures:** headband · dumbbell · sweat drops · the waffle he is definitely about to eat.
**Does:** try, fail, enjoy failing, be everyone's favourite, carry the group physically.
**Doesn't:** succeed at fitness · be shamed for his body or his appetite · be a joke *about* being fat. He is beloved and he knows it. **The joke is willpower, never weight** — the audience starts at 10 and body-shaming is a hard fail.

### 2 · Air Maxi — the ambitious modern hero
> *Wants to conquer the world, and is training for it.*

**Motivation:** Being great at it. Going further than last time, and being the one who shows it can be done.
**Energy 5/5 · Warmth 3/5** — warmth reads as **encouragement**, not affection.

> **He is not selfish and he is not rude.** *(Corrected by Jawat, 24 Aug 2026.)* His ambition points at **a standard, never at a person.** He competes with the bar, with yesterday, with himself — not with his friends and never at their expense. He raises the level and expects company. **Nothing possessive: no "mine", no "me first", no claiming a thing before the others get it.**
**Interests:** sports, fitness, rap culture, parties.

**Movement & silhouette.** Fast, sharp, athletic. Explosive starts. He is the **motion** of the group. Fox-grade cleverness in how he moves — reads a situation, then commits hard.

**What the poses establish.** His five hero poses are a power-fantasy arc, not observation: hands-on-hips with a cape · airborne fist raised · cape-planted stance · **screaming power-up inside an energy aura** · **planting a W flag on a summit.** He is a protagonist who goes after things — note that every one of those poses is aimed at **a summit, not at another character.**

**Signatures:** pink curl mass · **long rabbit-style ears** · **fox brush tail** · headphones worn *under* the ears · `AIR` / `MAXI` varsity jacket, `W` on the back · pink joggers · chunky sneakers.
*(Ears read rabbit, tail reads fox — see §6 Open items.)*

**Does:** compete, aim high, lead from the front, be charismatic, back himself out loud, **push the others to go further with him.**
**Doesn't:** be selfish · be rude · be possessive · gloat · put anyone down to raise himself · be humble to the point of passivity · lose interest · be a follower.

**Most developed character in the batch:** T-pose turnaround, five hero poses, expression sheet, full 3D sculpt.

### 3 · Mr Waffle — the glue
> *Not the strongest. The one who holds it together.*

**Reference:** Captain America in the Avengers — the moral centre, the one everyone defers to without being told to.
**Motivation:** The team. He wants the group to work.
**Energy 2/5 · Warmth 4/5**
**Interests:** football, gaming, sport generally.

**Movement & silhouette. He floats.** Reference movement: **R2-D2** — hovering, deliberate, small precise adjustments rather than strides. The 3D sculpt bears this out: the legs are short posts and the feet dangle with no ground-contact logic. He is the **stillness** of the group — in the hero illustration he is the only character standing upright, forward-facing and calm inside the chaos.

**Signatures:** round sunglasses · a bite missing from the top-left corner · drip glaze · bandaid on the leg · `MR WAFFLE` / `WAFFLE UP` embossed on the back · thumbs-up.
**Does:** steady the group, arbitrate, be reliable, be the one on the packaging, understate.
**Doesn't:** grandstand · panic · chase a trend · need to be the strongest.

**Brand role:** mascot and ambassador. He is the character who represents WaffleUp *to the outside*, even though Bhoppu is the centre of the fiction.

### 4 · Picchi — the curious child
> *Laughs easily. Cries just as easily. Draws everything.*

**Motivation:** Finding out. Every object is a question.
**Energy 5/5 · Warmth 5/5**

**Movement & silhouette.** Small, bouncy, unstable. Runs at things. Sits on the floor. The **smallest silhouette** in the cast, and the artwork uses that — she rides on top of Bhoppu in the hero illustration, smallest on largest.

**Emotional range is the point.** The expression sheet gives her sparkle-eyes, an open grin, a **wobbling teary face** and a **sleeping face.** She is the only character with a real emotional floor as well as a ceiling. Poses: lollipop · ice cream · alphabet blocks · tricycle · lying on the floor drawing · **cape-and-fists superhero stance.**

**Signatures:** huge pink curl mass · headband · freckles · W-pendant onesie · crayons and paper.
**Does:** wonder, explore, create, cry, be hungry, be sincere without irony.
**Doesn't:** be knowing · land a punchline · be cool · be cynical.

**Trajectory:** Picchi grows into an explorer. **Spacy is where that road ends.**

### 5 · Swirly — the loyal chaos companion
> *Dog energy. No words. Somehow always at the centre of the mess.*

**Reference:** dog-like — loyalty, enthusiasm, zero impulse control.
**Motivation:** Being with the others. Swirly follows.
**Energy 4/5 · Warmth 4/5**

**Movement & silhouette.** **Jumps, bounces, swirls.** Does not walk in a normal gait. Rounded candy-swirl body with no clear waist — the silhouette is a single rolling shape, which is why the movement reads as bouncing rather than striding.

**Communication.** **Swirly does not speak.** Cute sounds and reactions only. In written copy Swirly may carry a caption line, and **those lines are read as thoughts, not speech** — never as dialogue, never in a speech bubble, never spoken in video or animation. *(Confirmed by Jawat, 24 Aug 2026.)*

**Signatures:** pink-and-cyan candy swirl stripes · fangs · lolling tongue · seated pose.
**Does:** create harmless chaos, make messes, be surprisingly strong, be fiercely loyal, react hugely.
**Doesn't:** speak · plan · be malicious · be tidy.

### 6 · Merlulu — the sporty lion
> *Where the brand started, with the energy of where it's going.*

**Pronouns: he/him** — confirmed by Jawat, 24 Aug 2026. The next Surprise Card print batch carries "he" and the redesigned look.
**Motivation:** Belonging, and being good at things. Very close to Air Maxi in appetite, further from him in ego.
**Energy 4/5 · Warmth 4/5**
**Interests:** **badminton — at a professional level**, gaming, tech, fitness, sport, parties, music.

> **Two distinctions from Air Maxi, confirmed by Jawat 31 Aug 2026.** Merlulu is a **pro badminton player** — the only character in the cast with a sport of his own — and he is the **tech head** of the group. Air Maxi trains for everything and competes with the bar; Merlulu has one discipline he is actually excellent at, and a laptop. That is the difference, not ego.

**Redesigned Aug 2026, and redesigned again for the current pose set.** He is now drawn as a **lion — full stop.** The Merlion form is gone: no fish half, no scaled body, no fin, no water jet. The face is polished and human-like. **All previous Merlulu artwork is void**, including everything in `assets/characters/singapore/merlulu/` and the old sticker sheets. Current artwork is the five poses in `Character poses/` (`Merlulu Main`, `Merlulu 001`–`004`).

**Movement & silhouette.** A lion on two legs — athletic, low-centred, grounded. Crouches, leans, sits cross-legged. Explosive when he plays and completely still when he doesn't; the pose set runs from a badminton smash mid-air to sitting on the floor with a laptop. He is the closest character to camera in the hero illustration, crouching.

**Signatures:** flowing hot-pink mane · yellow-gold face and limbs · fangs and lolling tongue · **lightning-bolt shades** · **gold W medallion on a chain** · cyan headphones · cyan tee, pink shorts with cyan-and-white stripes, pink sneakers · cyan paw gloves · a bandaid on one knee · **a badminton racket with the W bolt on the head**.
**Does:** play badminton, compete for fun, socialise, game, tinker with tech, gather people, carry brand heritage lightly.
**Doesn't:** be timid · be sarcastic · be drawn as a Merlion or with any fish anatomy · be presented as Bangladesh-*origin* (Singapore is where he's from) · carry the heritage point heavily.

**Symbolic role:** Singapore is where Waffle Up Global was founded. Merlulu carries that, and should read as heritage worn as streetwear, not as a history lesson.

> ✅ **Resolved 24 Aug 2026.** The Singapore-only restriction is **void**. Merlulu is one of ten universal characters and belongs anywhere the others do — as the hero illustration already has him, front and centre. Singapore remains his **origin**, which is backstory, not a fence.

### 7 · Spacy — the future self
> *Picchi, older, from a timeline where she kept going.*

**Canon:** Spacy is a **future version of Picchi from an alternate timeline.** Teenager. More mature, more experienced, more independent than Picchi — but the same person underneath, and the artwork shows it: same face structure, same curl mass under the helmet.
**Motivation:** Going further. Picchi's curiosity, grown up and equipped.
**Energy 3/5 · Warmth 4/5**

**Movement & silhouette. Never on the ground.** Always floating, drifting or flying. This is a hard visual rule with no exceptions in finished work.

**Poses:** rocket cockpit · drifting weightless with a waffle and a popsicle floating past · planting a W flag on an asteroid · spinning a ringed planet on one finger.

**Signatures:** space helmet with cyan side-pods · W chest badge · cyan-and-pink suit · peace sign · the pink curls visible through the visor.
**Does:** explore, float, be capable, be a little ahead of everyone.
**Doesn't:** touch the ground · be as naive as Picchi · be an adult.

**Handle with care:** the Picchi link is a genuine story asset — the same character at two ages, in two timelines. Do not spend it on a throwaway post.

### 8 · Stovy — the guardian
> *Carries the name, and takes that seriously.*

**Motivation:** Protection. He stands between things and the people he's responsible for.
**Emotional signature:** **controlled anger** — present, held, never released carelessly.
**Energy 3/5 · Warmth 3/5**

**Movement & silhouette.** Heavy, planted, deliberate. Squares up rather than dodges. **Can grow dragon wings**, and **may descend by parachute** (W-branded). The body is the WaffleUp oven/box with a `#` on the side panel.

**Powers, drawn:** dragon wings · a flame-breath weapon · a spiked horn · a scowl · the parachute descent.

**Signatures:** the box-oven body · `WAFFLE UP` on the lid · fire · the `#` panel.
**Does:** protect, stand against injustice, be cautious, be dependable, be the one you send in.
**Doesn't:** lose his temper carelessly · start a fight · be cruel · be reckless.

**Hard limit:** Stovy is the character most likely to drift toward food-safety, hygiene, allergen or halal territory, because he is an oven. Per `CLAUDE.md` §3, **those subjects stay literal and never carry a joke or a character.**

### 9 · Tvy — the rebel
> *Anti-boring. Anti-bedtime. Anti-being-told.*

**Motivation:** Freedom. Nobody tells Tvy what to do.
**Energy 4/5 · Warmth 3/5**
**Reference energy:** Bumblebee-style rebel — defiant, fearless, on your side.

**Register — confirmed by Jawat, 24 Aug 2026: playful mischief.** Tvy's rebellion is **anti-boring, anti-rules, anti-going-home**, never political. The graffiti is a W tag. The protest is refusing to leave. The oppression being fought is homework and bedtime. **No real-world political framing, no organised resistance imagery, no cause.** The audience starts at 10 and this is a food brand.

**Movement & silhouette.** Restless, kinetic, never at rest for long. Skates, climbs, dodges. The one relaxed pose — floating on a donut pool ring in shades — reads as *lounging in defiance*, not as calm.

**Poses:** spray-painting a dripping W tag, discarded can on the floor · slingshot drawn and aimed · skateboarding · gaming with a wired controller · the pool float.

**Signatures:** CRT head · bent antennae · X eyes · bandaid on the screen · lightning-bolt tee · spray can · skateboard.
**Does:** break small rules, tag things, game, refuse, be fearless, be funny about it.
**Doesn't:** carry a political cause · reference real politics, protest movements or authority figures · be genuinely destructive · be cruel · be gentle and nostalgic *(this was the previous, incorrect reading)*.

### 10 · Icy — the fragile adventurer
> *Goes everywhere. Survives almost none of it. Goes again tomorrow.*

**Motivation:** The next place. Travel for its own sake.
**Energy 4/5 · Warmth 4/5**

**Movement & silhouette.** Upright popsicle on a WaffleUp stick — **no legs to speak of, so movement is a hop, a lean, a wobble.** Physically fragile: melts in heat, cracks in cold, and keeps going regardless. That contradiction is the whole character.

**Poses — every single one is an environment that could destroy it.** Sunbathing under an umbrella, **visibly melting into the towel** · shivering and dripping in ski goggles, skis planted in snow · F1 cockpit, screaming · surfing with shades and tongue out · a straight-to-camera wink and point.

**Signatures:** pink-and-cyan swirl · the WaffleUp branded stick · shades · a drip that shouldn't be there yet.
**Does:** travel, brave things it shouldn't, stay optimistic, melt and carry on.
**Doesn't:** give up · be genuinely hurt · be a victim.

**Icy and Swirly.** Swirly sometimes overpowers Icy — this is **cartoon slapstick with instant recovery**, in the register of a big dog knocking over something small. Icy is never afraid of Swirly, never excluded, and always bounces back in the same beat. **It is never bullying, and it is never framed as one character having power over another.** *(See §6 — pending Jawat's confirmation of the intended register.)*

## 3. The relationship web

| Pair | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Bhoppu + Picchi** | The classic duo. Big and small, both open-hearted. Drawn structurally: Picchi rides on Bhoppu. |
| **Picchi → Spacy** | The same person, two ages, two timelines. The universe's one piece of real lore. |
| **Air Maxi ↔ Mr Waffle** | Ambition vs steadiness. Air Maxi charges; Mr Waffle holds the line. The healthiest tension in the cast. |
| **Air Maxi ↔ Merlulu** | Near-twins in appetite — sport, music, parties, headphones. **Air Maxi chases a standard; Merlulu builds a group.** Neither is selfish or rude — that is not the difference. Air Maxi trains for everything; **Merlulu is a pro badminton player and the cast's tech head** — one discipline, done properly. Air Maxi says *go further*; Merlulu says *come along*. |
| **Swirly + everyone** | Loyal to all, attached to no one in particular. The chaos that happens *to* a scene. |
| **Stovy → the group** | Protector. The others don't ask; he just stands there. |
| **Tvy ↔ Stovy** | Rule-breaker and rule-keeper. The cleanest comedy pairing in the cast, and completely unexploited. |
| **Swirly ↔ Icy** | Big and fragile. Slapstick only — see §2 Icy. |

**Three movement languages, no overlap:**
> **Bhoppu = mass · Air Maxi = motion · Mr Waffle = stillness**

Everyone else sits between them. If two characters are moving the same way in a frame, one of them is wrong.

## 4. Visual rules that carry over

Unchanged from `CLAUDE.md` §5 and `LOGO-RULES.md`, restated because they bind every brief built on this file:

- **Supplied artwork only.** No redraws, no recolours, no restyles, no new poses invented.
- **No AI generation of any character, ever.** Composite the supplied PNG over an AI background. This is the approved workflow and the only one.
- One character per piece, full colour, unmodified.
- **Positive moments only.** Never complaints, errors, outages or anything negative.
- Merlulu's previous artwork is **superseded** by the Aug 2026 redesign — check before using anything in `assets/characters/singapore/merlulu/`.

**New hard visual rules from this file:**
- **Spacy is never shown standing on the ground.**
- **Mr Waffle floats** — no committed weight on the feet.
- **Swirly never speaks aloud**, and never appears with a speech bubble.
