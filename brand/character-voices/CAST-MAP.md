| **Spacy** | Permanently amazed by scale | Tvy | As above. |

| **Merlulu** | Two things that shouldn't work, working | — | Singapore only. **Never one of the Bangladesh nine.** |

## The hyper-warm resolution

Bhoppu, Picchi and Icy all read "energetic and fun" from their bios. That's the collapse risk. They are split by **what the joy is about**:

> **Bhoppu = the place · Picchi = the moment · Icy = the crowd**

Same energy, three different sources. If a line would work for two of them, it isn't finished.

## Reading the audition

Every voice file uses the same six situations. **Read across characters, not down.** Cover the names — if you can't tell who's speaking, the voices aren't distinct enough yet.
