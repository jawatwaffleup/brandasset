# Air Maxi — Voice

**Status: DRAFT — awaiting approval** · Approved by: ______ · Date: ______
**Rewritten 24 Aug 2026.** Character source: `brand/CHARACTER-BIBLE.md` §2.
Card bio: *"The beat of the street! With his paw on the pulse of the latest trends, he's the go-to guy for all things trendy in the city."*

> ⚠ The previous version read him as a **scout** — someone who spots trends but never sets them. That was too small. His pose sheet is capes, power-ups and planting a flag on a summit. He is a protagonist who goes after things — **after a goal, never after someone else's.**

## 1. Voice in one sentence
Aiming higher than the situation requires, and expecting you to come with him.

## 2. Personality and energy
Confident, clever, competitive, charismatic. Ambition is the engine — he wants to be great and says so without embarrassment. Fox-grade smart: reads the room fast, then commits hard.

**His ambition points at a standard, never at a person.** He is not selfish and he is not rude. He competes with the bar, with yesterday, with himself — not with his friends, and never at their expense. The charisma is real: people follow him because he makes them want to try harder, not because he is above them.
**Energy 5/5 · Warmth 3/5** — his warmth shows up as **encouragement**, not affection.

## 3. Vocabulary, catchphrases, language mix
- First person, forward-leaning. Goals, targets, next.
- Competitive framing aimed at **the bar, not at people** — beat it, top that, next level, personal best, watch this.
- Brings people with him: come on, you too, keep going, that's it.
- Sport, music and party references. Rap cadence is on-brand; keep it current.
- Quick bursts. Fragments are fine.
- Bangla/English code-switching freely. He is the most street-fluent voice in the cast.
- **Pronouns: he/him.** Card bio ("his paw").
- No approved catchphrase. **Don't invent one.**

## 4. May / may not
**May:** compete, aim high, back himself out loud, call his shot, lead from the front, **push other people to go further.**
**May not:** **be selfish** · **be rude** · be possessive about anything (no "mine", no "me first", no claiming a thing before others get it) · be dismissive of the others · put anyone down to raise himself · gloat · be humble to the point of passivity · use dated slang · be nostalgic · claim a real record, ranking or superlative the business hasn't verified. **"Best in Bangladesh" is a banned claim, not a character trait.**

> **The line to hold:** if a line makes him sound like he wants *more than* someone else, it's wrong. He wants **further than he got last time.** Confirmed by Jawat, 24 Aug 2026.

## 7. Nearest neighbour
**Merlulu.** The tightest collision in the cast — both sporty, headphoned, party-coded, pink-and-gold, and since the roster unified on 24 Aug 2026 they can share a frame.

> **Air Maxi is chasing a standard. Merlulu is building a group.**

**Neither is selfish and neither is rude** — that is not the difference. Air Maxi's energy points at **the goal** and pulls people along behind it. Merlulu's points at **the people**, and the activity is the excuse. Air Maxi says *go further*; Merlulu says *come along*. If a line would work for either, it isn't finished.

Second neighbour, **Mr Waffle**: ambition vs steadiness. Air Maxi charges. Mr Waffle holds the line.
