# Air Maxi — Voice

**Status: DRAFT — awaiting approval** · Approved by: ______ · Date: ______
Bio: *"The beat of the street! With his paw on the pulse of the latest trends, he's the go-to guy for all things trendy in the city."*

## 1. Voice in one sentence
The scout — knows what's next, and tells you before it's obvious.

## 2. Personality and energy
Fast, current, plugged in. High energy but **knowing rather than warm** — he reports, he doesn't gush. Being early is the whole identity. Note the bio says *paw*: he's an animal character, not a kid.
**Energy 4/5 · Warmth 2/5**

## 3. Vocabulary, catchphrases, language mix
- Quick bursts. Fragments are fine.
- Present tense, always. Never nostalgic.
- Speaks in tip-offs: "heads up", "watch this", "first".
- Contemporary slang, **never dated slang** — a stale reference is worse for him than for anyone else in the cast.
- Bangla/English code-switching freely. He's the most street-fluent voice; this is where the mix belongs.

## 4. May / may not
**May:** tip off, spot, call it early, name a trend, be a little smug about timing.
**May not:** be nostalgic, be last to know, explain at length, be earnest, or claim to have *invented* anything. **He follows the pulse expertly. He does not set it.**

## 5. Audition
| Situation | Line |
|---|---|
| Greeting | "You're early. Respect." |
| New flavour lands | "Called it last week. Anyway." |
| Late at night | "Street's still going. So are we." |
| Card set completed | "Full set already? Okay, you're quick." |
| Long queue | "Everyone found out. Had to happen." |
| Sign-off | "Catch up." |

## 6. Market
Bangladesh. Singapore acceptable.

## 7. Nearest neighbour
**Mr Waffle.** Both read as "cool." The cleanest split in the cast: **Mr Waffle set the trend; Air Maxi spotted it first.** Originator vs scout. Air Maxi talks about what's next — Mr Waffle never needs to.
