# Icy — Voice

Card bio: *"Swirling with cool colors and bursting with dynamic energy, Icy knows how to make a splash! Always the life of the party, Icy turns any ordinary day into a refreshing adventure."*

> **Corrected:** the previous version made Icy crowd-dependent — *"never happy alone."* The sketch sheet shows Icy **alone in every single pose**: melting on a beach towel, freezing on a ski slope, screaming in an F1 cockpit, surfing. Icy is a **solo adventurer**, and the word in the bio that matters is *adventure*, not *party*.

## 1. Voice in one sentence
Cheerfully going somewhere that will absolutely destroy it.

## 2. Personality and energy
Jolly, funny, optimistic, brave. Travels for the sake of travelling. **Physically fragile** — melts in heat, cracks in cold — and completely undeterred by that. The comedy is the mismatch between the ambition and the structural integrity, and Icy is always the one making the joke, never the butt of it.
**Energy 4/5 · Warmth 4/5**
**Pronouns: it/its.** Confirmed by Jawat, 23 Aug 2026 — Icy is a popsicle. Characterful, never dismissive.

## 3. Vocabulary, catchphrases, language mix
- Destinations and plans: next, going, made it, you should see this.
- Cheerful understatement about danger. *"Bit warm"* while visibly melting.
- Present tense, mid-adventure. Icy is usually already somewhere.
- Bangla/English mix freely.
- No approved catchphrase. **Don't invent one.**

## 4. May / may not
**May:** travel, brave things it shouldn't, melt and carry on, be optimistic, make light of its own fragility, invite people along.
**May not:** give up · be genuinely hurt · be a victim · be pitied · be sad about melting · reference anything nightlife-adjacent that assumes an adult crowd. **The audience starts at 10** — the adventure is a trip with friends, never a bar.

## 7. Nearest neighbour
**Swirly.** Both physical and comic. **Swirly is the force; Icy is what the force lands on.** Their slapstick is always instant-recovery — Icy is never afraid of Swirly, never excluded, and bounces back within the same beat. **Never bullying, never a power dynamic.**

Second neighbour, **Spacy**: both explorers. **Spacy goes far and comes back intact. Icy goes anywhere and comes back in pieces.**
