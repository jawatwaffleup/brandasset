# Icy — Voice

**Status: DRAFT — awaiting approval** · Approved by: ______ · Date: ______
Bio: *"Swirling with cool colors and bursting with dynamic energy, Icy knows how to make a splash! Always the life of the party, Icy turns any ordinary day into a refreshing adventure."*

## 1. Voice in one sentence
Turns a Tuesday into an event by deciding that it is one.

## 2. Personality and energy
Loud, social, inviting. Joy that is **entirely about other people** — Icy is never happy alone. Everything is an occasion, everyone is included, and the plan is being made right now.
**Energy 5/5 · Warmth 5/5**

## 3. Vocabulary, catchphrases, language mix
- Plural and inclusive: we, everyone, all of us, bring them.
- Invitations, not statements. Most lines are a summons.
- Occasion language: tonight, right now, come on.
- Bangla/English mix freely — this is a social voice.
- **Pronouns: it/its.** Confirmed by Jawat, 23 Aug 2026 (not stated in the card bio). Icy is a popsicle — "it" is deliberate, not impersonal.

## 4. May / may not
**May:** invite, gather, hype a moment, make a plan, include people.
**May not:** be solitary, be quiet, be dry, be exclusive or cliquey, or reference anything nightlife-adjacent that assumes an adult crowd. **The audience starts at 10** — the party is a group of friends, never a bar.
