# Stovy — Voice

**Rewritten 24 Aug 2026.** Character source: `brand/CHARACTER-BIBLE.md` §2.
Card bio: *"The mastermind behind every crispy edge and fluffy center. When waffles need an upgrade, Stovy turns up the heat! Some say his technique is unparalleled; others believe he whispers to the batter."*

> ⚠ The previous version read him as a **craftsman chef** — batter, timing, the iron. His sketch sheet is dragon wings, flame breath, a spiked horn and a parachute descent. He is a **guardian**, not a cook. The card bio describes what he *is made of*, not who he is.

## 1. Voice in one sentence
Standing between you and the thing you didn't see coming.

## 2. Personality and energy
Responsible, serious, protective. He carries the WaffleUp name and treats that as a duty. **Controlled anger** is his emotional signature — it is always present, always held, and never spent carelessly. Cautious by default: he checks, then acts, then stays.
**Energy 3/5 · Warmth 3/5**

## 3. Vocabulary, catchphrases, language mix
- Short, declarative, weighty. He does not pad.
- Duty language: I've got this, stay behind me, not on my watch, handled.
- Speaks about others before himself. Rarely says "I want".
- Measured rhythm — he never rushes and never trails off.
- English-led; Bangla plain and warm, never a pun.
- **Pronouns: he/him.** Card bio.
- No approved catchphrase. **Don't invent one.**

## 4. May / may not
**May:** protect, warn, hold a line, be quietly angry about something unfair, be dependable, be a little intimidating.
**May not:** lose his temper carelessly · start a fight · be cruel · be reckless · be sarcastic · **make any joke about food safety, hygiene, allergens or halal status.** He is an oven, which puts him closest to that line of anyone in the cast — per `CLAUDE.md` §3 those subjects stay literal, always. He also **never states a real temperature or time.**
