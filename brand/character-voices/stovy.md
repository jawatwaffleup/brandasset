# Stovy — Voice

**Status: DRAFT — awaiting approval** · Approved by: ______ · Date: ______
Bio: *"The mastermind behind every crispy edge and fluffy center. When waffles need an upgrade, Stovy turns up the heat! Some say his technique is unparalleled; others believe he whispers to the batter."*

## 1. Voice in one sentence
The craftsman who cares enormously how it's made, and assumes you do too.

## 2. Personality and energy
Calm, precise, quietly proud. Slightly mystical about method — he talks about batter the way a chef talks about dough. He is the only voice permitted to be technical.
**Energy 2/5 · Warmth 4/5**

## 3. Vocabulary, catchphrases, language mix
- Specific and concrete: heat, edge, centre, timing, the grid, the batter.
- Precision where it's true. **Never invented numbers.**
- Talks to the equipment and the batter as collaborators.
- Measured rhythm. He doesn't rush.
- English-led; Bangla plain and warm.

## 4. May / may not
**May:** explain craft, take pride, be precise, be a little mysterious about technique.
**May not:** be careless, rush, be sarcastic, dismiss the process, state a temperature or time that isn't real, or **make a food-safety, hygiene, allergen or halal joke.** Per `CLAUDE.md` those stay literal — and Stovy is the voice most likely to wander near them, so the prohibition lives here.

## 5. Audition
| Situation | Line |
|---|---|

| New flavour lands | "Took a while to get the edge right. It's right now." |

## 6. Market
Bangladesh and Singapore.

## 7. Nearest neighbour
**Mr Waffle.** Both calm and low-energy. **Stovy is earnest about craft; Mr Waffle is indifferent to effort.** Stovy cares how it's made. Mr Waffle only notices that it's good.
