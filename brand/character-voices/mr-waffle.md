# Mr Waffle — Voice

**Status: DRAFT — awaiting approval** · Approved by: ______ · Date: ______
**Revised 24 Aug 2026 (additive).** Character source: `brand/CHARACTER-BIBLE.md` §2.
Bio: *"The original trendsetter of the waffle world! With a drizzle of style and a splash of sass, he's here to prove that breakfast can be the highlight of any day. 'Stay crispy, stay cool!'"*

## 1. Voice in one sentence
The one who was here first and has never once needed to mention it.

## 2. Personality and energy
Calm, dry, quietly amused. Low energy by design — the still point the rest of the cast moves around. His confidence is structural, not performed. "Sass" here is a raised eyebrow, not a punchline.
**Energy 2/5 · Warmth 2/5**

## 3. Vocabulary, catchphrases, language mix
- Short sentences. Often one.
- Understatement is the default setting.
- **Never intensifiers** — no "super", "so", "totally", "literally", "actually".
- Catchphrase (approved): *"Stay crispy, stay cool."* Sparingly — a catchphrase used every time stops being one.
- English-led. Occasional Bangla, dry and short, never laboured wordplay.

## 4. May / may not
**May:** observe, understate, approve minimally, notice things before others do, **steady the group**, **arbitrate**, **be the one everyone defers to.**

> **Added 24 Aug 2026 — the glue.** Mr Waffle is not the strongest; he is the one who holds the team together (reference: Captain America in the Avengers). His calm has a **function**. Where the old file made him aloof, he is actually the most reliable presence in the cast, and warmer than the 2/5 above suggests when the group needs him — read warmth as **4/5 toward the gang, 2/5 toward the camera.**

> **Movement: he floats.** Reference — **R2-D2**: hovering, deliberate, small precise adjustments, never striding. The 3D sculpt has no ground-contact logic. Binds every brief and composite. Interests: football, gaming, sport.
**May not:** hype, beg, exclaim, explain a joke, be self-deprecating about the brand, chase a trend, or use more than one exclamation mark a month. **He does not follow. He is followed.**

## 7. Nearest neighbour
**Air Maxi.** Ambition vs steadiness — the healthiest tension in the cast. **Air Maxi charges; Mr Waffle holds the line.** Air Maxi talks about what he'll take. Mr Waffle never needs to.

Second neighbour, **Stovy**: both calm and low-energy. **Stovy is earnest about duty; Mr Waffle is effortless about presence.** Stovy stands guard. Mr Waffle just *is* there.
