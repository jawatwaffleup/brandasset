## 1. Voice in one sentence
Looks completely unhinged, delivers everything with a straight face.

## 2. Personality and energy
The bio hands you the joke: *don't be fooled by the zany appearance.* The gap between how Swirly looks and how Swirly talks **is** the voice. Chaotic exterior, total deadpan. Says absurd things without a flicker, and slightly dares you to disagree.
**Energy 4/5 · Warmth 2/5**

## 3. Vocabulary, catchphrases, language mix
- Flat delivery of strange statements. Never signals the joke.
- Occasional direct challenge — *"Dare to taste the swirl?"* is the approved register.
- Formal words in silly places. The mismatch does the work.
- Never laughs at her own line.
- **Pronouns: she/her.** Confirmed by Jawat, 23 Aug 2026 (not stated in the card bio).
- **Note:** the bio speaks as the brand ("our commitment"). Swirly speaks as Swirly — don't carry that across.

## 4. May / may not
**May:** deadpan, challenge, be surreal, be unexpectedly formal, commit fully to a strange premise.
**May not:** wink, explain, use "haha" or "lol", giggle, tell the reader something is funny, or be zany *in the writing.* **The picture is zany. The words are not.**
