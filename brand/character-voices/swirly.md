## 0. The rule

**Swirly communicates through sounds and reactions. Never words, never aloud.**

| Medium | What Swirly may do |
|---|---|
| Video, animation, audio | **Sounds only.** No dialogue, ever. |
| Speech bubbles | **Never.** No bubble, no tail, no quotation marks. |
| Written caption | **Allowed — as thought, not speech.** Confirmed by Jawat, 24 Aug 2026. |

**Pronouns: she/her.** Confirmed by Jawat, 23 Aug 2026 (not stated in the card bio).

## 1. Character in one sentence
A loyal, bouncing mess who is thrilled you're here and has already knocked something over.

## 2. Personality and energy
Dog-like: loyal, adventurous, mischievous, playful, zero impulse control. Surprisingly strong. Creates **harmless** chaos — she never means damage, and damage is never the point of the gag. Attached to the group rather than to any one character.
**Energy 4/5 · Warmth 4/5**

## 3. Sound and reaction vocabulary
- Short, round, non-verbal noises — a delighted *mmf*, a questioning *mrrp*, a disappointed *pfff*. **Never words, never onomatopoeia that spells a word.**
- Reactions land in the **body**: ears, tongue, tail-equivalent motion, the whole silhouette bouncing.
- **Movement is the punchline.** Jumping, bouncing, swirling — never a normal walking gait.
- Timing: Swirly reacts a beat *before* everyone else and a beat *too much*.

## 4. Written captions — the thought rule
When Swirly carries a written line, it reads as **what Swirly is thinking**, not what Swirly is saying.

- Present tense, no quotation marks, no speech bubble.
- Simple and immediate — she thinks in wants and reactions, not sentences with clauses.
- Short. If it needs a comma, it's too long.
- Italics or a caption bar are fine. **A speech bubble is not.**

| Situation | Thought caption |
|---|---|
| Greeting | *you came back* |
| New flavour lands | *that one. that one right there* |
| Late at night | *nobody said go home* |
| Card set completed | *all of them. every single one* |
| Long queue | *waiting is very hard actually* |
| Sign-off | *again tomorrow* |

## 5. May / may not
**May:** bounce, react hugely, knock things over, be loyal, be strong, be thrilled, think out loud in a caption.
**May not:** **speak** · appear with a speech bubble · plan anything · be malicious · be tidy · be sad for longer than a beat · be used for a complaint, error or apology.

## 6. Market
**Universal.** All ten characters work in every market (unified 24 Aug 2026).

## 7. Nearest neighbour
**Icy.** Both physical, both comic. **Swirly is the force; Icy is the thing the force happens to.** Their slapstick is always instant-recovery — see `CHARACTER-BIBLE.md` §2 Icy. It is never bullying and never a power dynamic.

Against the rest of the cast Swirly has **no collision risk at all** — she is the only non-speaking character, which does the separation on its own.
