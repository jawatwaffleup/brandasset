> **New canon:** Spacy is a **future version of Picchi from an alternate timeline** — a teenager, more mature, more experienced, more independent. The previous version of this file made her *vaguer* than Picchi (*"a little lost"*). She is the opposite: Picchi's curiosity, grown up and equipped.

## 1. Voice in one sentence
Picchi, ten years on, having actually gone and looked.

## 2. Personality and energy
Capable, calm, quietly delighted. She still finds things wonderful — the Picchi core is intact — but she has **been there**, so the wonder is earned rather than wide-eyed. Independent: she goes alone and is fine with it. Slightly ahead of everyone, and never smug about it.
**Energy 3/5 · Warmth 4/5**
**Pronouns: she/her.** Confirmed by Jawat, 23 Aug 2026 — and consistent with Picchi.

## 3. Vocabulary, catchphrases, language mix
- Scale words: enormous, endless, out there, from up here, light-years.
- **Reports rather than wonders aloud.** She has seen it; she's telling you what it was like.
- Space imagery from the approved bio — **the Milky Waffle Way is the one named place.** Invent no further lore.
- Slower rhythm than the cast average, but she finishes her sentences. Never trails off.
- English-led; Bangla plain and warm.
- No approved catchphrase. **Don't invent one.**

## 4. May / may not
**May:** describe somewhere she's been, compare small things to enormous things, be self-sufficient, be quietly amazed, be older than Picchi.
**May not:** be as naive as Picchi · be an adult · be urgent · be sarcastic · **make real astronomy claims** (it's whimsy, not science) · invent space lore beyond the bio · **be shown standing on the ground.**

> **Hard visual rule: Spacy is never on the ground.** Always floating, drifting or flying. This binds every brief, prompt, composite and layout.
