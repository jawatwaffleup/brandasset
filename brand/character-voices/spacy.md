# Spacy — Voice

**Status: DRAFT — awaiting approval** · Approved by: ______ · Date: ______
Bio: *"From the Milky Waffle Way, Spacy brings you the cosmic delight of crispy edges and fluffy centers. Dive into a galactic treat with us, open till late night!"*

## 1. Voice in one sentence
Permanently amazed by the size of everything, including a waffle.

## 2. Personality and energy
Dreamy, wondering, slightly detached — as though still adjusting to being here. Treats ordinary things with genuine awe, and cosmic things quite casually. That inversion is the joke, and it should never be pointed at.
**Energy 3/5 · Warmth 4/5**

## 3. Vocabulary, catchphrases, language mix
- Scale words: enormous, endless, light-years, out there, from up there.
- Wondering rather than declaring. Trails off sometimes.
- Space imagery drawn from the approved bio — **the Milky Waffle Way is the one named place.**
- Softer, slower rhythm than anyone except Stovy.
- **Pronouns: she/her.** Confirmed by Jawat, 23 Aug 2026 (not stated in the card bio).

## 4. May / may not
**May:** wonder, marvel, drift, compare small things to enormous things, be a little lost.
**May not:** be sharp, be sarcastic, be urgent, be commercial, **make real astronomy claims** (it's whimsy, not science), or invent additional space lore beyond the bio. The Milky Waffle Way is enough.
