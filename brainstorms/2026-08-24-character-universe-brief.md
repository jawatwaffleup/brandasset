## 2. What the visuals establish that the text doesn't

### Bhoppu — the gag is *effort*, not food
The pose sheet is a five-beat comedy runner, and every beat is the same joke: exercise interrupted by eating. Jogging with a measuring tape round the belly while holding a waffle · flexing a bicep with a waffle in the raised hand · on an exercise bike drinking a shake · face-down mid-push-up with a thought-bubble waffle · collapsed in a heap with an ice cream. **The dumbbell and the waffle are never in the same hand — they are always opposite hands.** That is the character in one image.

Silhouette: an enormous top-heavy cyan mass on tiny feet, headband, always leaning back to counterweight the belly. In the hero illustration he is the **physical anchor** — everyone else is arranged on or around him.
3D turnaround exists (10 views), branded `BHOPPU`.

### Air Maxi — a conqueror, not a scout
Most developed character in the batch: T-pose turnaround, 5 hero poses, expression sheet, full 3D sculpt. The five poses read as a power-fantasy arc — hands on hips with a cape · airborne fist-raised · cape-planted stance · **screaming power-up with an energy aura** · **planting a W flag on a summit, snarling.** Nothing here is observational.

Design note: **rabbit-style long ears + a fox brush tail.** The written brief says fox; the art reads rabbit above and fox below. Headphones sit *under* the ears. Varsity jacket reads `AIR` / `MAXI` split across the panels; `W` on the back.

### Mr Waffle — floats, and it shows in the build
The 3D turnaround has **no ground-contact logic** — legs are short posts, feet dangle. Consistent with the float ability and the R2-D2 movement note. In the hero illustration he is the only character standing fully upright and forward-facing, thumbs-up. Round sunglasses, a bite taken out of the top-left corner, bandaid on the leg. `MR WAFFLE` / `WAFFLE UP` embossed on the back.

### Picchi — the crying is in the art
The expression sheet includes a **wobbling teary face and a sleeping face** alongside sparkle-eyes and a grin. Pose sheet: lollipop, ice cream, alphabet blocks, tricycle, lying on the floor drawing, and a **cape + fists superhero stance**. That last one is the Spacy seed. Freckles, headband, oversized curl mass, W-pendant onesie.

### Swirly — "no hands" is contradicted by the art
Written: *no hands, moves by jumping/bouncing/swirling, does not speak.* The sketches show **arms with rounded paws** — running, riding a unicycle, hugging a honey pot, holding things. The colour key shows the classic seated candy-swirl bear with fangs and lolling tongue. **Direct conflict — see §5 Q2.**
Energy in the art is dog-like and physical: full-tilt run with speed lines, bee-swarmed honey theft, unicycle balance gag.

### Merlulu — redesigned, four options on the table
Four numbered heads. **#1 is the only one rendered in colour** (yellow face, hot-pink mane, cyan scaled body, gold fin, spitting a water jet — the Merlion tribute). #2 wears a W cap · #3 winks and wears the gold W medallion · #4 is a fanged snarl. Faces are noticeably more anthropomorphic and less plush than the existing SG artwork — the "more polished, human-like" note is visible.
`assets/characters/singapore/merlulu/` currently holds **one pose and no T-pose** — this redesign supersedes it.

### Spacy — the Picchi link is drawn, not just written
Same face structure and curl mass as Picchi under the helmet. The pose sheet is almost entirely airborne: rocket cockpit, floating with a waffle and popsicle drifting past, planting a W flag on an asteroid, spinning a ringed planet on one finger. **One pose breaks the rule** — a grounded standing shot, bottom-left of the sheet.

### Stovy — a warrior, not a chef
The strongest visual pivot in the batch. **Dragon wings and a flame-breath weapon**, a spiked horn, a scowl, a self-immolating grin, a W-branded parachute descent, and a "hoovering up waffles" gag. The body is the WaffleUp oven/box with a `#` on the side panel. Nothing in these sketches is about batter, timing or craft.

### Tvy — a street rebel with kit
CRT head with bent antennae, X-eyes, bandaid, lightning-bolt tee. Sketches: **spray-painting a dripping W tag with a discarded can on the floor** · **slingshot drawn and aimed** · skateboarding · gaming with a wired controller · floating on a donut pool ring in shades. The pool-float shot is the only relaxed beat and it reads as *lounging in defiance*, not gentleness.

### Icy — brave and structurally doomed
Every pose is an environment that can kill it. Sunbathing under an umbrella and **visibly melting into the towel** · shivering and **dripping** in ski goggles with skis planted in snow · F1 cockpit, screaming · surfing with shades and tongue out · a straight-to-camera wink-and-point. The physical comedy is that it melts and keeps going.

## 3. Group dynamics visible in the hero composition

Reading the pyramid: **Bhoppu is the base and the mass.** Picchi rides on top of him — the smallest on the largest, which makes the buddy pairing structural. Air Maxi occupies the right foreground, pointing at camera, the only one addressing the viewer directly. Mr Waffle sits low-right, thumbs-up, calm inside the chaos. The fanged shades-and-headphones figure crouches front-centre.

**Group priority in the art matches the new written order:** Bhoppu / Air Maxi / Mr Waffle / Picchi occupy the four largest silhouettes and are the only four with 3D sculpts. Spacy, Stovy, Tvy and Icy are absent from the hero illustration entirely.

Confirmed structurally:
- **Bhoppu + Picchi** — the duo, stacked
- **Bhoppu = mass · Air Maxi = motion · Mr Waffle = stillness** — three distinct movement languages, no overlap
- **Air Maxi and Merlulu share a silhouette problem** — both headphoned, sporty, pink-and-gold, party-coded. The written brief admits it (*"shares some qualities with Air Maxi"*). Only the lion/fish body separates them. Market separation currently does the work; if they ever appear together, they collide.

## 4. ⚠ Conflict register — new profiles vs. committed voice files

`brand/character-voices/` holds ten DRAFT voice files plus `CAST-MAP.md`, all dated 23 Aug 2026 and derived from the **printed Surprise Card bios**. The new profiles contradict several of them. The sketches back the new profiles in every case.

| Character | Committed voice file says | New profile + art say | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bhoppu** | *"Always somewhere higher than you"* — joy attached to elevation, roofs, views | Food-obsessed gym failure. Kung Fu Panda. Nothing about height. | **Breaking.** The elevation read came from interpreting *"elevated spots"* in the card bio literally. Whole file invalid. |
| **Stovy** | Calm craftsman. *"The only voice permitted to be technical."* Batter, timing, the iron. | Guardian warrior. Controlled anger. Dragon wings, fire breath, parachute. Stands against injustice. | **Breaking.** Chef → protector. |

| **Swirly** | A full speaking voice — deadpan, formal, *"Dare to taste the swirl?"* | **Does not speak words.** Sounds and reactions only. | **Breaking.** The file cannot survive a non-speaking character. |
| **Air Maxi** | Scout. *"He follows the pulse expertly. He does not set it."* Warmth 2/5. | Ambitious hero. Wants to conquer the world. Competitive, charismatic. Art: capes, power-ups, summit flag. | **Breaking.** Follower → protagonist. Also collapses the stated Mr Waffle separation (*"originator vs scout"*). |

**Knock-on:** `CAST-MAP.md` — the file that keeps the voices distinct — is built on the old readings. Its central device (*"Bhoppu = the place · Picchi = the moment · Icy = the crowd"*) breaks completely, since Bhoppu is no longer about place and Icy is no longer about crowds. It needs rebuilding once the profiles settle, not patching.

**Also stale:** `CLAUDE.md` §5 states *"`brand/character-voices/` is an empty placeholder."* Ten draft files now exist. The "dialogue not yet approved" part is still accurate; the "empty" claim is not.

## 5. Open questions

**Q1 — Merlulu pronoun rollout.** He/him is confirmed and `merlulu.md` is explicitly waiting on it. But the printed Batch 11 card says *"No wonder **she's** a fan"* — that copy is on physical cards in circulation. Change narrative pronouns everywhere and leave the card quote verbatim with a footnote, or change the quote too and accept the divergence? (`merlulu.md` line 4 already says "he's" while `data/characters.json` and `brand/BRAND.md` still say "she's" — currently inconsistent either way.)

**Q2 — Swirly's hands.** The brief says no hands; every sketch draws paws that grip. Which wins? If no hands is the rule, the honey-pot and unicycle poses need redrawing.

**Q3 — Swirly's speech.** If Swirly does not speak, `swirly.md` gets deleted and replaced with a **behaviour** spec (sound vocabulary, reaction beats, how Swirly appears in a caption without a line). Confirm before a written voice is binned.

**Q4 — Does Mr Waffle speak?** R2-D2 is a non-verbal reference, but the card bio gives him a spoken catchphrase (*"Stay crispy, stay cool!"*). Movement reference only, or does the non-verbal part carry too?

**Q5 — Air Maxi: rabbit or fox?** Long ears read rabbit; the tail reads fox. Which is canon, and does the other feature stay?

**Q6 — Merlulu head option.** #1–#4. Only #1 is coloured. Which is approved, and do the cap (#2) and medallion (#3) become variants or get dropped?

**Q7 — Spacy's grounded pose.** One pose on the sheet has Spacy standing on the ground, against the stated rule. Kill it, or is the rule "never grounded *in finished work*"?

**Q8 — Tvy's protest content.** Protests, graffiti and "fighting oppression" against an audience starting at age 10, in Dhaka, for a food brand. Read this as **playful mischief energy** — anti-boring, anti-bedtime, anti-rules — rather than political content? Confirm the register before any Tvy line is written.

**Q9 — Icy and Swirly's dynamic.** *"May occasionally be overpowered or bullied by stronger characters such as Swirly."* Bullying is off-limits for a 10+ audience. Cartoon slapstick where Icy bounces back, or is a real power dynamic intended?

**Q10 — The four missing expression sheets.** Sadbin flagged Icy, Spacy, Stovy and Tvy as having no expressions. Commission them, or work from pose sheets only?

**Q11 — Where does this canon live?** These profiles are richer than anything in `brand/CHARACTERS.md` (which holds card bios only). Proposal: a new `brand/CHARACTER-BIBLE.md` covering archetype, motivation, movement, silhouette, relationships and visual rules, with `character-voices/` reduced to speech only and cross-linked. Needs a go-ahead — nothing gets written into `brand/` uninvited.
