## Summary / key decisions

_(running synthesis — updated after every answer)_

**Objective (settled, Q1).** The commercial job is **frequency** — make the existing customer base buy more often. Base awareness is explicitly **not** the target: "people know that we exist."

**Second, standing objective (Q1).** Be **consistently active on social media**. Jawat raised this unprompted, which means always-on presence has value to him beyond whatever frequency it drives. Not yet established whether social is a *means* to frequency or an *end in itself* — resolve before building the content plan, because it determines whether every post needs a CTA.

**Channel position (corrected at Q2).** WaffleUp is **not Foodpanda-dependent**. Direct sales channels exist and **outlet sales do not depend on Foodpanda**. "We cannot sideline Foodpanda" means *don't abandon or de-prioritise it* — it does **not** mean Foodpanda is the only conversion surface. Jawat's words: *"So no idea is getting killed."* Direct-order, own-delivery, WhatsApp and walk-in are all live and markatable. Treat channels as parallel, not hierarchical.

**Ruled out for now (Q1).**
- *New-outlet acquisition* — expansion "might take us a bit of time." Not a near-term driver. Don't build the plan around launches.

**Occasion baseline (settled, Q2).**
- **Evening through to late night is the rush window** — not late-night only. I had over-narrowed it; corrected.
- **Typical repeat cycle is weekly.** A small number of **daily** repeat customers also exist.
- **Peak days: Thursday, Friday, Saturday** in Bangladesh outlets. Singapore differs slightly but the core pattern holds. The rule is **local weekend + rush hours**, not a fixed weekday.
- Implication: the customer already comes on their own weekend. **The whitespace is Sunday–Wednesday in BD** (Mon–Thu in SG).

**Asset status.** The house audio track ("Sweet Night Bites", `assets/marketing/audio/`) is **outdated and will be replaced soon**. Do not build campaigns on it. The *night-time core theme* it expresses is still correct.

**Repeat is segmented, not uniform (Q3).** *"The repeat traits vary in different customers."* At least two behaviours coexist:
- **Habit buyers** — weekly, plus a thin daily layer.
- **Treat buyers** — monthly, or once in 2–3 months. These are genuine occasion purchases.
Do not plan against a single average customer. The frequency job is different for each: habit buyers need a *reason today*; treat buyers need their *occasion to come round more often*.

**HARD CONSTRAINT — no discounts (Q3).** *"we dont give discounts - that would be against our brand image."* This is a brand-integrity rule, not a budget preference. It rules out: percentage-off, BOGO, happy hour, weekday offers, flash sales, price-led win-back, discount codes with creators. **Every frequency mechanic proposed in this project must create value without cutting price.** The Surprise Card is the existing model of how to do that — give something, don't discount something.

**Targeting directive (Q3).** Do **not** split the plan into weekday vs weekend. Run frequency across the **whole week**. And this is a planning stance, not a message — *"you dont have to mention that we are targeting whole week, its implicit."* Never say it in creative.

**Capacity: headroom exists (Q3).** *"even our rush hours are within our capacity. We can take more customers."* No throughput ceiling at peak. This kills my argument that weekend marketing would be self-defeating — deepening the peak is safe, which is what makes the whole-week stance correct.

**Weekday demand is an assumption, not a finding (Q3).** *"I didnot try much on weekday push, but Its easy guesswork.. people are less likely to come on weekdays."* Untested. Worth a cheap experiment later; not worth building the plan on either way.

**Still open.** AOV / basket-building and brand-equity-for-expansion were offered as options and not addressed either way.

### Q3 — Where should the extra visit land?
- **Asked:** Recommended aiming frequency work at the Sun–Wed trough rather than the Thu–Sat peak, on three grounds: it's the only net-new volume, incremental margin is higher on a fixed cost base, and pushing a peak that's already queueing destroys experience. - **Captured:**
  - **Recommendation overruled — correctly.** Jawat: *"I would like not to focus on only weekday or weekend, rather in general whole week."* Plus: *"you dont have to mention that we are targeting whole week, its implicit"* — i.e. it's a planning stance, never a message.
  - **Capacity has headroom:** *"even our rush hours are within our capacity. We can take more customers."*

- **Flags:**
