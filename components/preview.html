Even spacing in all directions — at least the height of the “W” on every side. Nothing enters the hatched zone.

Do & don't

✓ Do

  • Keep the signature wave inside the letterforms
  • Even clearspace on all four sides
  • RGB on screens, CMYK in print
  • White icon on colour, RGB icon on white
  • Logo ≥120px, icon ≥32px

✗ Don't

  • Uneven spacing or cropped-out corners
  • Solid coloured letters without the wave
  • Single coloured letters without the wave
  • Stretch, rotate, recolour, or drop the ®
  • Rebuild it by hand — use the supplied file

11 — PRODUCT

Menu

Two price books. The same item costs differently at a standard outlet and at Chef's Table — Chef's Table runs +16% to +33% on the same SKU, and the gap is not uniform. That is why price_book is mandatory in every schema.

Prices are POS-verified (Aug 2026) — standard from the GiantSoft till, Chef's Table from the ZAB export. They are not read from menu artwork, which was stale by up to two revisions: 12 prices in the previous version of this kit were wrong. Menu artwork is a design record, not a price book. Recipes and ingredient quantities exist nowhere in the brand archive — recipe is null for every SKU.

The range is not flat. Nutella and Tri-Chocolate are roughly two-thirds of all units sold. Don't build forecasting, stock priority or shelf space that assumes an even catalogue.

12 — PRODUCT

Outlets

15 trading sites, four outlet types, three price books, eight channels. Nothing should assume a single shop. This table is the ops team's register, not the website locator — the two disagree.

IDNameTypeCityPOSStatusFoodpandaCharacter

POS today is fragmented: GiantSoft at all 8 standard outlets, ZAB (Chef's Table's own system) at the 4 CT sites, Foodpanda at the cloud kitchens. Otomatic ran historically between two GiantSoft eras — any trend line crossing that switch compares two different definitions of a sale.

Four things that trip people up: (1) Foodpanda is not a cloud-kitchen thing — every standard outlet has a storefront; the cloud kitchens are just the two with nothing else, and they pay ~4 points more commission. (2) Chef's Table has no delivery at all. (3) Rampura is not open yet. (4) Banani trades under a different legal entity and BIN (CLOUD) to every other site, so consolidated revenue and VAT must split on legal_entity.

There is currently no single source that gives a correct company revenue number — Foodpanda overlaps GiantSoft, ZAB is CT-only, and Banani is a separate entity. See waffleup-marketing-kit/data/POS-AND-REPORTING.md.

Automation note

Serials like FLL-9525656492 live only in Excel workbooks. There is no redemption system — so nobody knows which codes have been used, and a photographed code can be redeemed twice. A small redemption service is the highest-ROI build available.

The 2026 data confirms it: five sites distributed 5,989 cards and logged zero redemptions; distribution is recorded for four months while redemption is recorded for eight, so the published "7.36% redemption rate" is not a rate; and the export's own dates are transposed. At the till the programme appears as two zero-value complimentary categories — SURPRISE CARD for issue, SURPRISE CARD ITEM for redemption. Preserve that distinction in any schema.
