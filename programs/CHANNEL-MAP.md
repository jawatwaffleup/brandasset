## 5. Claude's read

*Not part of the supplied map. Six observations, in order of how much they cost if ignored.*

### 5.1 "Unlock free items" is a hard fail waiting to leak

Phase 1 of the tech block says **"unlock free items."** The word *free* is banned in WaffleUp copy in any language on any channel (`CLAUDE.md` §3, confirmed by Jawat 23 Aug 2026). This is an internal sheet, so no harm done yet — but wording on an internal map is where product copy, POS strings and app microcopy get drafted from. It should read **"unlock rewards"** before anyone builds Phase 2 against it.

The scratch card exists precisely so value arrives as *luck*, never as a markdown. Naming it "free" undoes the whole mechanism.

### 5.2 It's an inventory, not a plan

Every box is a channel; almost none carry an owner, a cadence or a priority. The only prioritisation on the whole page is the small activities strip — DO FIRST / NEXT / HOLD / LIVE / TO DEFINE. That strip is the most useful thing on the map and it's the smallest. Two of six items say "TO DEFINE," and they happen to be the two that decide everything else: **customer acquisition** and **loyalty & retention**.

### 5.3 WhatsApp is missing

`brand/BRAND.md` lists WhatsApp orders on **+880 1958 095887** as a live ordering route. It isn't on the map. Either it's been retired `[CONFIRM]` or the delivery block is incomplete.

### 5.4 The map flattens two distinctions the CTA depends on

- **Chef's Table has no delivery.** The delivery block reads as though every site is on every app. Anything built from this map will eventually put a Foodpanda CTA on Chef's Table content.
- **Singapore is a separate market**, not just "the market where Grab applies." Different price book, different character (Merlulu), different everything.

### 5.5 Reputation channels are hygiene, not content

TripAdvisor, Wikipedia, Reddit, Crunchbase, LinkedIn and Glassdoor sit in the same visual weight as Instagram. They shouldn't compete for content time. They are **check-quarterly, fix-if-wrong** surfaces — six of them together are less than one week of Instagram. Worth splitting them out so nobody builds a content calendar that tries to feed thirteen channels.

### 5.6 Two live channels the Social Engine doesn't cover

`programs/SOCIAL-ENGINE.md` is built for Instagram, TikTok and Facebook. The map also claims **Snapchat** (filters, geofencing) and **YouTube** (content, characters). Neither has a format in the engine.

Snapchat geofencing around outlets is genuinely interesting for the Surprise Card — a filter that only unlocks inside the outlet is a scarcity-of-a-*thing* play, which is allowed, where a price-drop play is not. YouTube already has cover art at `assets/marketing/audio/YTCover.png` and three cuts of *Sweet Night Bites*, but that audio has **no verified commercial-rights record** and cannot go public without human clearance.

### The one thing I'd do with this map

Fill in the two **TO DEFINE** cells. Customer acquisition and loyalty & retention are the objectives every other box on this page is supposed to serve, and until they're defined the map can only ever be a list of places, not a plan. The Surprise Card guideline already does most of the loyalty half — it just hasn't been promoted onto this page.
