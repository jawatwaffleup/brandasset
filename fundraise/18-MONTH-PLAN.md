# The 18-month plan

Three stages: **get raisable** (4 months) · **raise** (~5 months, overlapping) · **deploy** (18 months post-close).

> The 18 months is the **post-close operating plan** — the standard investor reading of "USD 1M buys 18 months of runway to hit X." It is not 18 months from today. Getting to the close realistically takes until **Q2 2027**, and a plan that pretends otherwise fails on its first milestone.

**Every currency figure here is illustrative and unsourced.** They express *proportion and priority*, not budget. Replace each with a costed number before this reaches an investor.

## Stage 1 — The raise · Dec 2026 – Apr 2027

Overlaps the tail of readiness. Assume **five months from first meeting to cash**, and it often runs longer.

| Phase | Weeks | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Soft circle** | 1–3 | Five to eight friendly conversations. Not asking for money — asking what breaks. Rewrite |
| **Outreach** | 4–8 | Warm intros, in waves. Never approach every target at once; each wave teaches you something for the next |
| **First meetings** | 6–12 | Aim for 30–40 qualified conversations to close one round. This is normal, not failure |
| **Second meetings & diligence** | 10–16 | The appendix does the work. Response speed is itself a signal |
| **Term sheet** | 14–18 | Ideally more than one. Optionality is the only real leverage a founder has |
| **Legal & close** | 18–22 | Two jurisdictions. Budget more time than feels reasonable |

**Run it as a process, not a sequence.** Parallel conversations create the timeline pressure that gets a round closed. Sequential conversations let it drift for a year.

**Track everything:** every conversation, stage, objection and pass. The pass reasons are the most valuable data you will get — if three funds pass for the same reason, that reason is real and the deck is wrong.

## The metrics that unlock a Series A

Anchor the whole plan on these. If they are true in Q6, the next round is straightforward.

| Metric | Why it is the one that matters |
|---|---|
| **Franchised outlets trading** | Proves strangers can run it |
| **Premix revenue as % of total** | Proves you are not a restaurant |
| **Premix gross margin at volume** | Proves the model's engine |
| **Franchisee payback period** | Determines whether the pipeline ever fills |
| **Franchisee-referred pipeline %** | The purest signal the model works |
| **Product consistency, franchised vs owned** | Validates the premix thesis outright |
| **International outlets trading** | Proves it travels |
| **One reconciled revenue number, automatically** | Proves the company is run properly |

## Use of funds — proportion, not budget

| Line | % | Illustrative | What it buys |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Premix production facility & line** | 25–30% | ~$250–300k | The plant. **The largest line, because it is the thing that makes you not a restaurant** |
| **International beachhead — GCC** | 15–18% | ~$150–180k | Entity, regulatory, halal, first Dubai site. **Reduces sharply if the master-franchise partner co-invests — pursue that first** |
| **Franchise system build & rollout** | 13–15% | ~$130–150k | Manual, training academy, franchise sales, legal, franchisee support |
| **Key hires** | 12–14% | ~$120–140k | Finance lead, food scientist, franchise director — 18 months |
| **R&D, food science & IP** | 8–10% | ~$80–100k | Formulation work, shelf-life, variants, filings across markets |
| **Technology — POS/ERP/loyalty** | 8–10% | ~$80–100k | One source of truth. Kills the reconciliation problem permanently |
| **Working capital & inventory** | 8–10% | ~$80–100k | Raw materials, packaging, buffer stock |
| **Contingency** | ~5% | ~$50k | Because something will cost more than this table says |

**Two things to notice, and to say out loud in the room.** The plant is the biggest line — that is the slide that proves the reframe is real, not rhetorical. And the technology spend is not back-office overhead: your own Vision names **procedures and processes**, so building the system that produces one true revenue number is on-thesis, not admin.

## What breaks this plan

| Risk | Early warning | Response |
|---|---|---|
| **Reconciliation drags past Dec** | Month 2 with no methodology agreed | Bring in outside accountants immediately. This blocks everything |
| **Lab result is weaker than expected** | First report | Reposition on consistency and cold-chain economics — still a strong pitch. **Better to find out now than in diligence** |
| **Formulation is not patentable** | Counsel's first opinion | Trade secret plus brand plus system. Know which layer you rely on before you are asked |
| **Round takes longer than 5 months** | Normal. Assume it | Do not let the readiness work decay while raising. Keep the business growing — traction during a raise is leverage |
| **Franchisees underperform** | First cohort's numbers | Stop selling franchises. Fix the system. **A bad early franchisee cohort poisons the pipeline for years** |
| **Plant delays** | Permitting or equipment lead times | Contract-manufacture in the interim. Do not let franchise rollout wait on your own building |
| **FX or repatriation blocks a foreign investor** | Raised in first meetings | Have the structural answer ready on day one, not improvised in the room |
